# ADR 0008: The remaining Phase 2 items, and the landing page's rain

- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-09-30
- **Deciders:** the owner ("let's work on all of the unbuilt items"; "make the token rain the main background
  of the landing page … a randomizer that can display thousands of different words/numbers"), and Claude
  for the details

## Context
ADR 0007 deferred a list of Phase 2 items. The owner asked for all of them, and then for the token rain to
fill the landing page, made of thousands of words and numbers instead of one sample reply.

## Decisions

### Built from ADR 0007's deferred list
- **Remappable shortcuts (WCAG 2.1.4).**
  - The keymap is stored in the preferences (`src/components/prefs/keys.ts`). Settings has an editor:
    choose an action, then press the new key. Esc cancels.
  - A key taken from another action says so. Tab, Esc, Enter, and modifier chords are reserved.
- **Glossary popovers** (`src/content/glossary.ts`, `src/components/glossary/Term.tsx`).
  - Copy marks a term with `term(id, text)`, a new kind of `SourcedText` part.
  - The definition opens on hover (after a short delay) or focus, and pins on click. It closes with Esc,
    stays open while hovered, and never takes focus (WCAG 1.4.13).
  - Screen readers get it as the term's description. Definitions carry claims like any other copy.
- **"Is this real?" (Simple) and "How do we know this?" (Detailed).**
  - Every `<Datum>` and Example frame now carries `data-source` and `data-detail`. The panel
    (`WhereFrom`) reads the labels the current step actually rendered.
  - It lists exactly what is on screen: by kind at Simple depth, and one row per source field at Detailed.
- **The three lanes** (`src/stages/lanes.ts`, `Lanes.tsx`).
  - Each step belongs to This app, OpenAI's service, or Inside a model like this.
  - Simple depth shows a one-line ticker. Detailed depth lists the reply's recorded events per lane, with
    measured durations. Each duration is taken from one clock only, and the time inside OpenAI's service is
    never split into phases (CLAUDE.md L4, A6).
- **Detailed network sub-scenes.**
  - "Where each token sits": the same token at two positions has identical numbers going in.
  - "Each position on its own": the feed-forward step.
  - "Show another example pattern" steps through three rule-based patterns: previous-token,
    duplicate-token (Wang et al. 2022, claim C052), and induction (Olsson et al. 2022, claim C053).
    Each pattern is introduced on a neutral sample. The repeat-based ones appear on your tokens only when
    your message repeats a token.
  - Detailed depth now has more steps than Simple. Every Simple step still exists, in order, so changing
    depth keeps your place.
- **Simulated picks.** In the temperature What-if, 20 weighted random picks are drawn on the page at the
  chosen temperature. They're labeled "simulated on this page; the model wasn't asked again".
- **Exercises** (in the panels, offered beside the chapters). Answers stay on the page.
  - "Guess the likely option": real close calls from your reply, shown before the percentages.
  - "Real or example? A quick check": seven things from the walkthrough, sorted into real data or examples.
- **The end step's reply text,** formatted or as written: formatting is just characters the model wrote.
- **Real-speed replay** (in "How long did it take?"): the reply reappears on the arrival times this browser
  measured, labeled as including the network. It's a small player of its own, started by the viewer: the
  one exception to "views have no timers" (CLAUDE.md §7), outside the walkthrough's timeline.
- **The first-visit tour** of the chat page, in three steps. It is server-rendered, then hidden before paint
  once seen (`THEME_INIT_SCRIPT` sets `data-tour`). Settings can show it again.
- **Also added:** token counts on each message card in "What gets sent", and close calls outlined on the
  end step's full-reply tape.

### The landing page's rain
- **A full-page background.** The rain now fills the window behind the landing page.
  - Every block of text sits on a solid card, so the rain never runs behind the words (CLAUDE.md §8).
  - The pause control and the caption are in the first card.
  - It holds one still frame under reduced motion, more contrast, or Effects off.
- **Thousands of real tokens.** The pool is `src/content/rain-tokens.json`, generated by `npm run rain`:
  - 3,055 whole-word tokens (each with its leading space), found by tokenizing this project's own writing;
  - the numbers 0 to 999, each a single token.

  Sampling the whole vocabulary was rejected because it would include offensive tokens. A test checks that
  each entry is exactly one `o200k_base` token, so "real tokens" stays true (claim C055). The rain picks
  from the pool at random and shows words without their leading space.

### Speed
The additions first pushed the slowest interaction to 528 ms on the throttled phone profile.
- The cause: the whole walkthrough re-rendered on every pane switch, and twice per step because of the
  announcement.
- The fix: the walkthrough is memoized, with a stable callback, and step announcements live in their own
  small component.

| Measure | Now | Before the fix |
|---|---|---|
| Pane switch | 200 ms | 392 ms |
| Next step | 64 ms | 256 ms |
| Chapter jump | 72 ms | 296 ms |
| `/sample` worst interaction | 160 ms | 528 ms |
| LCP, all pages | about 0.9–1.0 s | — |
| CLS | 0 | — |

## Consequences
- **Tests.** 41 end-to-end tests (12 new, axe included) and 208 unit tests. The new unit tests cover the
  keymap, the attention patterns, simulated picks, and the rain pool.
- **Still open from the plan:**
  - linked highlighting of a token across the chat bubble and the walkthrough;
  - picking another close call from the Hook card (the guess exercise covers other close calls);
  - an "Instant" playback speed;
  - the manual NVDA and VoiceOver pass (owner, before release).
