Blue light waves are shorter and scatter more than the longer wavelengths, like red or yellow. As sunlight passes through the atmosphere, the blue light is scattered in all directions, making the sky look blue to our eyes. This effect is more pronounced during the day when the sun is high in the sky. During sunrise and sunset, the light passes through more atmosphere, scattering shorter wavelengths and allowing longer wavelengths like red and orange to dominate.

## 10. How does a refrigerator keep food cold?

A refrigerator keeps food cold using a refrigeration cycle. It contains a refrigerant that absorbs heat from inside the fridge. Here’s how it works:

1. **Evaporation**: The refrigerant evaporates in the evaporator coils, absorbing heat from the fridge interior.
2. **Compression**: The gas is then compressed by the compressor, raising its temperature and pressure.
3. **Condensation**: The hot gas moves to the condenser coils outside, releasing heat to the surroundings and condensing back into a liquid.
4. **Expansion**: The refrigerant then passes through an expansion valve, cooling down before re-entering the evaporator.

This cycle continuously removes heat, keeping the inside of the refrigerator cold.
