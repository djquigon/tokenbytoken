## 3. How do vaccines work?

Vaccines train your immune system to recognize a germ—such as a virus or bacterium—without making you go through the full disease. They expose the body to a harmless form or piece of the germ, or instructions for making one.

Your immune system responds by producing antibodies and immune cells. Some of these cells remain as “memory,” so if you encounter the real germ later, your body can recognize and fight it faster, lowering your chance of getting sick or becoming seriously ill.

Vaccines can cause mild, temporary effects like a sore arm or fever as your immune system responds.

## 10. How does a refrigerator keep food cold?

A refrigerator doesn’t create cold so much as move heat out of its insulated interior.

A circulating refrigerant absorbs heat inside the fridge as it evaporates. A compressor then squeezes the refrigerant, making it hotter and higher-pressure. In coils at the back or underneath, the refrigerant releases that heat into the room and condenses back into a liquid. It then passes through a narrow valve, cools, and returns inside to absorb more heat.

A thermostat switches the system on and off to keep the interior near the set temperature.

Imagine a set of Russian nesting dolls. You open one doll and find a smaller doll inside. You keep opening dolls until you reach the tiniest one. Then you work your way back out.

In a computer program, a recursive function calls itself. It needs:
- **A stopping point**, called the *base case*, so it doesn’t go on forever.
- **A smaller step**, which brings it closer to that stopping point.

For example, to count down from 3: say “3,” then count down from 2, then 1, and stop at 0.

## 14. What causes the seasons on Earth?

Earth’s seasons are caused by its axis being tilted about 23.5° as it orbits the Sun. As Earth travels around the Sun, each hemisphere alternately tilts toward or away from it. A hemisphere tilted toward the Sun gets more direct sunlight and longer days, producing summer; tilted away, it gets less direct sunlight and shorter days, producing winter. The seasons are **not** caused by Earth being closer to or farther from the Sun.
